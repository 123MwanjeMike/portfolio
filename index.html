About me

Full-Stack Developer

I am a Kampala, Uganda based technocrat with a 2 year practical experience in web design and web development. My aim is to offer individuals and businesses web products that show them that I really care!

My clients are my top priority and through my products I work hard towards developing long-lasting and meaningful relations with them by building products that meet their business needs.

Student

In the rapidly growing Web development field, I'm constantly improving my skill set to meet with modern day requirements. I'm currently a Catalyst course student at Refactory Uganda and a Bachelors of Science in Software Engineering degree student at Makerere University. These are roles that make me outstanding at what I do.
